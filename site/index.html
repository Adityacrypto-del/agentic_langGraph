FrostByte: agents that turn vaccine cold-chain rules into verified silicon

Write a vaccine-storage rule in plain English. FrostByte writes the Verilog, tests it with a real simulator, fixes its own mistakes from real compiler and simulator errors, then synthesizes it and estimates its power against a 100 nW budget.

Source on GitHub Run it yourself
Why it matters

Vaccines spoil invisibly

Vaccines must stay at 2–8 °C. Heat and accidental freezing both damage them, and nothing on the vial shows it. Monitors need tiny, always-on, coin-cell logic, but custom chip design is slow and specialist work. FrostByte supports that work with open-source tools; it does not replace sign-off by engineers or WHO/UNICEF guidance.
