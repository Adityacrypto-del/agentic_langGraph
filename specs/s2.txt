Design a vaccine-fridge alarm FSM. Inputs: clk; rst_n (active-low asynchronous reset); sample_valid (1-bit strobe); temp (signed 8-bit two's complement, 0.5 degC per LSB); ack (1-bit, an operator acknowledge, may be high in any cycle). Outputs: alarm (1 bit), state (2 bits), bad_count (4 bits). States: NORMAL=0, SUSPECT=1, ALARM_EXCURSION=2, ALARM_FREEZE=3. A reading is bad if temp < 4 or temp > 16. A reading is freezing if temp <= 0. Readings are evaluated only when sample_valid=1. From NORMAL: a freezing reading goes to ALARM_FREEZE; else a bad reading goes to SUSPECT with bad_count=1; else stay. From SUSPECT: a freezing reading goes to ALARM_FREEZE; a bad reading increments bad_count and, when bad_count reaches 3, goes to ALARM_EXCURSION; an in-range reading goes to NORMAL with bad_count=0. In either ALARM state, readings are ignored and alarm=1; when ack=1 in any cycle the state returns to NORMAL with bad_count=0. alarm=1 only in states 2 and 3. After reset the state is NORMAL and bad_count=0. Sampling clock is 32768 Hz. ack has no effect in NORMAL or SUSPECT. In the ALARM states bad_count holds its last value until ack. If ack=1 and sample_valid=1 in the same cycle while in an ALARM state, ack wins and the reading is ignored.
